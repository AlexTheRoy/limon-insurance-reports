Demographic profile of all 1,763 populated California ZIP codes: household income, homeownership, home values, age and vehicles, built from the U.S. Census Bureau's ACS 2020–2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), the most current ZIP-level data published. You write home insurance statewide, so the whole state is covered, with your office market called out throughout: section 02 is a close-up of the Corona area (Corona, Norco, Eastvale, Jurupa Valley and the city of Riverside), and the wider Inland Empire (Riverside and San Bernardino counties) is compared with the state in every section. Los Angeles, Orange and San Diego counties, next door and the source of about a third of your Home leads, are shown alongside. Section 08 lines the state up against the 5,135 GOAL Home leads and 281 matched Home sales from August 4, 2025 to October 15, 2026. The companion spreadsheet carries every ZIP and every column; the wildfire and earthquake report covers the risk geography.

Data & files

  • SourceU.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020–2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), tables B01002, B01003, B08201, B11001, B11007, B19013, B25001, B25002, B25003, B25004, B25077. Geography: 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas.
  • Coverage1,763 populated California ZIPs. PO-box-only ZIPs have no resident population and no Census profile; traffic from them resolves to the surrounding ZIP. Median income is published for 1,591 ZIPs and median home value for 1,600.
  • Top-codingThe Census caps medians at “$250,000 or more” for income (17 ZIPs) and “$2,000,000 or more” for home value (68 ZIPs); those ZIPs are shown at the cap.
  • GeographyEach ZIP is assigned to the California county holding the largest share of its land (2020 ZCTA-to-county relationship file); 5 ZIPs whose land lies mostly in Nevada are left out. The 58 counties are grouped into ten regions: Los Angeles County, Orange County, the Inland Empire (Riverside and San Bernardino), San Diego & Imperial, Ventura & Central Coast, the Bay Area, the Sacramento Region, the San Joaquin Valley, Sierra & Eastern California, and the North State & North Coast. The city column is left blank: no city file was used, so ZIPs are identified by county.
  • LeadsAll GOAL prospects for this account, Aug 4, 2025 to Oct 15, 2026 (5,135 Home leads (standard and mobile home), plus 41 Auto leads that these pages leave out), counted by ZIP. 5,114 of 5,135 Home leads matched a populated California ZIP. Sales are leads whose phone number matched a sale in the policy files you uploaded (281 Home sales; 280 in a populated ZIP). Only counts by ZIP are used; no names or contact details appear here or in the spreadsheet.
  • PrecisionAll values carry ACS margins of error and are strongest for larger ZIPs. Blank means not published, never zero.
  • FilesLimon-CA-ZIP-Data-10-19-26.csv · every ZIP, every column, with the wildfire and earthquake tier from the companion report.
